## 1. Visual Direction & Security UX

The WORKZEN authentication suite has been completely upgraded to the **NovaDesk split-screen standard**:
- **Left Brand Panel:** Deep midnight navy gradient (`#0A0F1D` to `#1E1B4B`) with subtle ambient violet diffusion, lightning brand emblem, clear enterprise capabilities list (unlimited projects, role-based controls, 24/7 priority support), and social proof avatar stack with 5 gold stars.
- **Right Form Panel:** High-contrast crisp light form card with floating labels, password visibility eye toggles, primary gradient CTA button, social login options (Google, Microsoft, GitHub), and explicit legal agreements.
- **OTP & Verification UX:** Distinct 6-digit numeric input boxes with auto-advance, clear 15-minute countdown clock, and one-click resend triggers.
