## 1. Visual Direction & Brand Copywriting

The WORKZEN public marketing website has been completely redesigned around a luminous, enterprise-grade dark aesthetic inspired by modern developer platforms and high-converting SaaS benchmarks.

### Key Pillars:
- **Atmospheric Hero:** Deep indigo/midnight gradient canvas (`#0F1026` to `#07080E`) with ambient radial violet glow, bold headline (*"Simplify Your Workflow. Amplify Your Impact."*), and floating frosted-glass preview cards.
- **Genuine UI Representation:** The hero features authentic interface modules: sprint velocity telemetry, active issue status lists, and real-time team presence indicators.
- **Transparent Commercial Architecture:** Tiered pricing (Starter $0, Pro $12, Enterprise $29) with toggleable annual discounts, clear feature breakdowns, and zero fabricated testimonials or fake vanity metrics.
